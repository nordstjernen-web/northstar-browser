Privacy Policy

Nordstjernen does not collect personal data.

Effective date: June 14, 2026

Nordstjernen has no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts, no advertising ID use, and no background service that reports browser activity to the Nordstjernen project.

Local browser data

The browser may store normal browser data on your own device, such as history, cookies, cache, site storage, permissions, and settings. This data stays local unless you choose to send it somewhere.

Network use

Nordstjernen connects to the sites you visit, the resources those pages load, and the search engine you use. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions can be raised in the Nordstjernen issue tracker.
