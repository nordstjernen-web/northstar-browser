What is not here

No WebGL, no WebGPU, no AI web APIs, no telemetry, no crash reporter and no update pinger.

What decodes in-tree

PNG and APNG, GIF, BMP, JPEG and WebP through Wuffs; SVG in the engine; MP3, MP2 and optional Ogg for audio.

What carries the network

libcurl over HTTP/2, and HTTP/3 when the linked libcurl offers it, with HSTS, CSP, subresource integrity and cookies partitioned by origin.

Where the page runs

In the shell process. One window, one page, one process — no per-tab renderer, because there are no tabs.
