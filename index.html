nordstjernen

A north star, small and faithful — light enough to read by, slow enough to think with; built one line at a time.

Nordstjernen is an independent web browser written in C. Around 40,000 lines, small enough for one person to read in a long afternoon. No Blink, no Gecko, no WebKit, no JIT. No telemetry, ever.
